ProtonBridge is an unofficial, personal Android project focused on two-way folder sync (bisync) with Proton Drive. It builds with a pinned, reviewed rclone beta fork and incorporates Proton Drive patches originating from OSS Singularities’ Proton Drive Linux project.

* Folder bisync with preview, explicit initialisation and scheduled runs
* Rclone file management: browse, upload, download, move and rename files
* Support for other rclone cloud providers and encrypted remotes
* Android storage access and automatic dark theme

<b>DISCLAIMER: HEAVILY VIBE CODED.</b> This project was developed with extensive AI assistance by a maintainer with limited Android development experience. Use at your own risk, keep independent backups, and review the sync preview before initialisation. This is a personal project; issues, pull requests and feedback are welcome.

ProtonBridge is independent of and not affiliated with Proton AG. It is a fork of thies2005’s CloudBridge, which descends from Round Sync by Felix Nüsse, RCX by x0b and rcloneExplorer by patrykcoding. Original authors and GPLv3 licensing are credited in the repository.
